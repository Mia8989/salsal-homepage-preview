What your support funds

Where a supporter's backing goes.

01

Community programs

Free health screenings and educational events that catch peripheral arterial disease and diabetes-related wounds before they become a crisis.

02

Scholarships

Support for medical personnel pursuing wound care certification through the Harold L. Johnson Memorial Scholarship.

03

Patient assistance

Walking boots, compression socks, and diabetic shoes for patients working to protect a healing limb.

04

Ongoing programs and initiatives

Provider education, community outreach, and the day-to-day work of limb preservation advocacy.

About the foundation

A national 501(c)(3) nonprofit.

The Save a Leg, Save a Life Foundation is dedicated to education, advocacy, and communication in support of limb preservation. We work collaboratively across the healthcare system, with clinicians, patients, caregivers, industry partners, and community members, because amputation prevention takes all of us.
