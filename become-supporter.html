Membership matters

A direct, personal way to back the mission

Day in and day out, our supporters help us pursue every avenue to combat the obstacles standing between a patient and a saved limb. Becoming a Supporter is a direct, personal way to back that work, whether you are a clinician who sees the impact of preventable amputation firsthand, a patient or caregiver who has lived it, or a community member who wants to see fewer families face this loss.

What your support funds

Where a supporter's backing goes

C

Community programs

Free health screenings and educational events that catch peripheral arterial disease and diabetes-related wounds before they become a crisis.

S

Scholarships

Support for medical personnel pursuing wound care certification through the Harold L. Johnson Memorial Scholarship.

P

Patient Assistance

Medical supplies including walking boots, compression socks, and diabetic shoes for patients working to protect a healing limb.

O

Ongoing programs and initiatives

Provider education, community outreach, and the day-to-day work of limb preservation advocacy.

About the foundation

A national 501(c)(3) nonprofit

The Save a Leg, Save a Life Foundation is a national 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization dedicated to education, advocacy, and communication in support of limb preservation. We work collaboratively across the healthcare system, with clinicians, patients, caregivers, industry partners, and community members, because amputation prevention takes all of us.
