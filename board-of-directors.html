Board of Directors

The people behind the mission.

SALSAL's Board of Directors is made up of wound care physicians, nurse practitioners, researchers, and industry leaders who volunteer their time because they have seen how many amputations did not have to happen.

Donate Read our mission ›
Founder

Dr. Desmond Bell

Dr. Desmond Bell founded the Save A Leg, Save A Life Foundation in 2015. As a wound care specialist, he saw patients and caregivers repeatedly go without the information they needed to preserve a limb, even as the technology to prevent amputation kept advancing. He built SALSAL to close that gap, and the foundation's operating philosophy, "Prevention Through Action, Not Words," is his.

2026 board additions

Five new directors, five new strengths

Each 2026 addition brings a different piece of the limb-preservation puzzle: clinical science, governance, industry leadership, direct patient care, and clinical operations.

Shannon Clayton

Board Member

PhD-trained biomedical scientist and medical writer specializing in wound healing, immunology, and precision medicine, with postdoctoral experience at UC Davis. Co-author of the Time Out Before Amputation (TOBA) initiative.

Alex de Verteuil, PhD

Board Member

Life sciences strategy and operations leader. She has led a national team of Senior Field Applications Specialists at Leica Biosystems and supported commercialization at Abcam, bringing a systems-oriented perspective to governance and sustainable growth.

Luke Etter

Board Member

Healthcare industry leader and entrepreneur committed to improving outcomes for patients living with chronic wounds. Founder of Advanced Solution LLC, serving as its CEO through April 2026 before transitioning to Chairman of the Board.

Angelina Ferguson, DNP

Board Member

Family Nurse Practitioner and Certified Wound Specialist. Co-Founder and Chief Research Officer of Sygnola Research and Director of Clinical Research at MedCentris Wound Healing Institute, with numerous randomized controlled trials in diabetic foot and venous leg ulcers.

Cyndi Gilliam

Board Member

Clinical affairs executive with more than 15 years across advanced wound care, medical devices, and national clinical leadership. A recognized clinical thought leader and international speaker in advanced wound care.
