Financial partnership makes it all possible

Your investment goes directly where the need is greatest

Our corporate partners enable programs including the Patient Assistance Program and the Harold L. Johnson Memorial Scholarship. When your organization sponsors The Save A Leg, Save A Life Foundation, our Board of Directors determines the most effective use of funds, so your investment goes directly where the need is greatest.

Unrestricted financial sponsorships let us plan further ahead, run more screening events, and grow our advocacy work with confidence. Industry members committed to changing amputation statistics are welcomed as true collaborators, not just funders.

What your organization's support builds

D

Direct patient impact

Your support funds diabetic shoes, wound-care supplies, and screenings that catch disease before amputation becomes the only option.

P

Provider education at scale

Sponsorship funds scholarships and training that sharpen limb-salvage skill across the healthcare system.

V

Visibility with purpose

Your organization is recognized alongside a foundation trusted by clinicians and patient communities nationwide.

S

Board-directed stewardship

Unrestricted gifts are allocated where they will do the most good, reviewed and approved by our Board of Directors.
