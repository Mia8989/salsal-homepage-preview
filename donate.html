Every dollar funds
direct, measurable work.

Peripheral arterial disease and diabetes-related wounds send hundreds of thousands of Americans toward amputation every year, and a meaningful share of those amputations are preventable with earlier diagnosis and proper care. Save A Leg, Save A Life exists to close that gap.

01
Why your gift matters

A free screening. A certification. A pair of diabetic shoes.

Every dollar you give funds direct, measurable work: a free screening that catches disease before it becomes a crisis, a wound-care certification that sharpens a provider's skill, a diabetic shoe or compression sock that keeps a healing wound from becoming an emergency.

SALSAL is committed to taking action versus talking about the problem of often preventable lower extremity amputation. Dr. Desmond Bell, SALSAL Founder
Where your donation goes

Educate. Advocate. Connect.

Educate

Educate

We train physicians, nurses, and healthcare providers on evidence-based measures to prevent or delay non-traumatic amputations.

Advocate

Advocate

We stand behind people with limb loss and the populations most at risk, pushing for policy that puts limb preservation ahead of default amputation.

Connect

Connect

We build communication between the specialists, primary care providers, and community health workers who treat people at risk for amputation, so patients do not fall through the cracks between them.
