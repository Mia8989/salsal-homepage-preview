How we help

Early diagnosis and treatment save legs and lives.

Four programs, one goal: catch preventable amputations before they happen.

Donate Get involved ›
Program one

Provider education

Early diagnosis and treatment can save legs and lives, and that starts with the providers doing the diagnosing. SALSAL runs local screening events, networking conferences, and teaching opportunities that raise awareness of Peripheral Arterial Disease, Critical Limb Ischemia, and preventable lower extremity amputation among the clinicians who see at-risk patients first.

This information is for educational purposes only and is not intended to replace professional medical advice. Always consult your healthcare provider.

Program two

Harold L. Johnson Memorial Scholarship

The American Board of Wound Management Foundation administers this scholarship, funded by the Save A Leg, Save A Life Foundation, to help healthcare providers earn their wound certification. Every provider who becomes wound-certified through this scholarship is another clinician equipped to catch a preventable amputation before it happens.

Learn about the scholarship ›
Program three

Patient assistance

For patients actively working to prevent an amputation, SALSAL provides direct support: diabetic shoes, dressing supplies, knee walkers, and other wound-healing items. These are the practical tools that keep a treatable wound from becoming a surgical decision.

This information is for educational purposes only and is not intended to replace professional medical advice. Always consult your healthcare provider.

Learn how you can support this program ›
Program four

Community screening

SALSAL hosts free screening and education events throughout the United States, reaching the at-risk communities that face the steepest barriers to care: lack of caregiver support, financial constraints, transportation gaps, and simply not knowing what questions to ask. Each event pairs patient screenings with professional development workshops for the local healthcare providers who serve that community.
