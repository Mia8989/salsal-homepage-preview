Prevention through action, not words

Amputation is a
treatment failure.

Not a treatment option. SALSAL exists to make sure the evidence, the training, and the screening reach people before a limb does.

2015
Founded by Dr. Desmond Bell, a wound care specialist who kept seeing the same preventable outcome.
4
Programs running now: provider education, scholarship, patient assistance, community screening.
7
Mission partners funding the work, from Organogenesis to SweetBio.
How the mission runs

Three pillars.

01

Educate

We inform the public and healthcare professionals about evidence-based measures that prevent or delay non-traumatic amputations. That means webinars, conferences, fact sheets, and training that reach the clinician making the call.

Learning Center ›
02

Advocate

We stand behind patients with limb loss and the populations at highest risk, in the exam room and in policy.

03

Communicate

We build the lines between the professionals treating amputation-vulnerable patients, so care does not fall through the cracks.

Programs

Four ways that reaches real people

Provider education, the Harold L. Johnson Memorial Scholarship, direct patient assistance including diabetic shoes and wound supplies, and free community screening events.
