Why it matters

Free education keeps working long after the room empties.

Every fact sheet and every recorded session stays free, forever, so the evidence reaches the clinician or the patient who needed it months after the live moment passed.

For providers

We also teach this in person.

Provider education covers vascular screening before amputation, care team workflow, revascularization, the economic case, and real cases in limb salvage. It closes with the SALSAL Challenge, our ask that hospitals require vascular testing before approving an amputation. There is no fee to host a session.
