Every item in our store, from apparel to accessories, carries proceeds directly back into our programs. A simple way to support Save A Leg, Save A Life while spreading awareness of preventable amputation wherever you go.

Screenings, education, and patient supplies.

Purchases fund free community screenings, provider education, and the walking boots, compression socks, and diabetic shoes that help patients protect a healing limb.
