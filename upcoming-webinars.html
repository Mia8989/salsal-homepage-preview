How Our Webinars Work

Every SALSAL webinar is free to attend and open to clinicians, patients, and caregivers alike.

Register once, and you will receive the link ahead of the live session. Can't make it live? Every webinar is recorded and added to the Resource Library shortly after, so the education keeps working long after the call ends.

Next live webinar

Early Recognition and Treatment of PAD

September 16, 2026

A live panel on catching peripheral arterial disease earlier, before it becomes a limb-threatening emergency. This session covers the early signs providers and patients can watch for, and the treatment pathways that keep more options on the table.

Speakers

Dr. Olamide Alabi, Dr. Benjamin Zwierzchowski, and Dr. Anuj R. Shah.

This information is for educational purposes only and is not intended to replace professional medical advice. Always consult your healthcare provider.
