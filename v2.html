The premise

Most amputations are not inevitable.

They are a decision.

We exist to make sure it is the right one.

The stakes
0%
of lower-extremity amputations happen without prior vascular testing.
Source: SALSAL provider education. A time-out and a test can change the outcome.
What we do

Three fronts,
one limb at a time.

01

Educate

Provider training on vascular screening, revascularization, and the care-team workflow that catches risk early.

Provider education ›
02

Advocate

The SALSAL Challenge asks hospitals to require vascular testing before approving an amputation. A time-out that cannot be undone.

The SALSAL Challenge ›
03

Communicate

Free community screenings, plain-language resources, and a public library of recorded expert sessions.

PAD Awareness Month · September

The White Sock Campaign

One white sock on an exposed leg draws the eye to the lower limb, and to the patients who cannot afford something as basic as a pair of diabetic socks.

A SALSAL original, now moving through the patent process.

Established 2018 with ABWM

Harold L. Johnson Scholarship

Ten wound-certification exam scholarships a year, moving clinicians toward CWCA, CWS, and CWSP credentials. Preference for honorably discharged veterans.

Named for a Vietnam veteran who gave this foundation years of his life.
